\textbf{\bi{Summary}{摘要}}

\bi{
A port can own advanced software and still lose track of a container. The maturity of its data and analytics system therefore depends on whether people, technology, and governance deliver reliable information across operational handoffs. We develop an auditable framework for Intercontinental Cargo Moving (ICM) Corporation that connects measurement to investment decisions without requiring confidential records to leave the company.

Because the problem provides no corporate observations, we distinguish a deployable assessment system from a reproducible synthetic demonstration. A coverage-constrained screening model selects ten high-frequency indicators from a dictionary of 16, retains mandatory recovery and governance checks, and covers all nine business requirements. The illustrative collection cost falls from 29 to 17 units. Event identifiers, independent audits, missing-data bounds, and versioned rules preserve the link between each score and its evidence.

The Lag-aware Capability Filter (LCF) estimates people, technology, and process capability from monthly audit counts. Its empirical-logit observation model accounts for unequal sample sizes and workload; delayed actions enter the state equation only once. Across 48 simulated months, the test-period capability RMSE is \StateRMSE, compared with \RawRMSE\ for the direct monthly estimate. One-step prediction RMSE falls from \ZeroRMSE\ without an onset delay to \ForecastRMSE\ with validation-selected delays. However, nominal 90\% conditional intervals cover only \Coverage\% of test states. This failure makes uncertainty recalibration a deployment requirement.

The Chain Reliability Maturity (CRM) model translates capabilities into the probability of timely, error-free import and export handoffs. Nonnegative capability effects, a people--process interaction, and a shared-outage term make the score operationally interpretable. In 300 independent synthetic test profiles, the two Brier scores are \BrierImport\ and \BrierExport. The final illustrative capability profile gives a reference maturity of \BaseMaturity/100; it is not an estimate of ICM's actual maturity.

The Robust Improvement Portfolio (RIP) model combines diminishing returns, implementation delays, dependencies, staffing, and quarterly budgets. Exhaustive enumeration compares 16,807 project menus and selects a \PlanCost{}-unit portfolio. Its worst planning-scenario net benefit is \WorstNet\ units, versus \EqualNet\ for equal allocation. Each unit represents USD 10,000. Under nominal conditions, reference maturity reaches \FinalMaturity/100. Wider, held-out uncertainty still produces a 5\% budget-violation rate, so the guarantee applies only to the declared planning envelope.

We specify a phased implementation, customer-facing reporting, and transfer rules for smaller ports, larger ports, and trucking companies. Shared identifiers and compatible handoff measures help both port and carrier distinguish the source of delays. Customer confidence rests on traceable evidence, disclosed uncertainty, and corrective action, rather than an unsupported maturity label.
}{
港口即使拥有先进软件，也可能无法及时定位集装箱。数据与分析系统的成熟度，取决于人员、技术和治理流程能否共同支持可靠的业务交接。本文面向洲际货运公司（ICM）构建可审计的评估与改进体系，允许企业在内部计算指标，而不向外部披露保密明细。

赛题没有提供企业数值观测，因此本文严格区分可部署的评价体系与可复现的情景演示。首先，在16项指标字典中，通过业务覆盖约束选取10项高频指标，保留恢复、安全与治理检查，覆盖全部9类业务需求；示例采集成本由29单位降至17单位。事件编号、独立复核、缺失边界和规则版本共同保证每项分数能够追溯到证据。

建立含滞后的能力滤波模型（LCF），使用月度考核计数估计人员、技术和流程能力。经验对数几率观测方程处理样本量差异与工作负荷，行动滞后仅在状态方程中计入一次。在48个月的模拟实验中，测试期能力估计RMSE为\StateRMSE，低于直接月度估计的\RawRMSE；一步预测RMSE由无起效延迟模型的\ZeroRMSE\ 降至\ForecastRMSE。然而，名义90\%条件区间仅覆盖\Coverage\%的测试状态，因此部署前必须重新校准不确定性。

建立业务链可靠性成熟度模型（CRM），把三维能力映射为进出口业务按时、无重大数据错误交接的概率。模型采用非负能力效应、人员与流程交互及共享故障项。在300个独立模拟测试画像上，两条业务链的Brier分数分别为\BrierImport\ 和\BrierExport。期末示例画像的参考成熟度为\BaseMaturity/100，该数值不代表ICM真实成熟度。

建立稳健改进组合模型（RIP），同时考虑边际收益递减、实施延迟、前置依赖、人力与季度预算。穷举16,807种项目菜单后，选定名义投入\PlanCost\ 单位的组合；最坏规划情景净收益为\WorstNet\ 单位，等额方案为\EqualNet\ 单位。每单位为1万美元。在名义条件下，成熟度提高到\FinalMaturity/100。更宽的留出情景中仍有5\%预算违约率，稳健保证只能解释为给定情景范围内的保证。

最后提出分阶段实施、客户报告、大小港口迁移及卡车运输企业适配方案。共享业务编号与交接指标可以帮助港口和承运商定位延误责任。客户信任应建立在可追溯记录、透明的不确定性与明确整改行动之上。
}

\pagepart{Introduction: measure the handoff, not the software}{引言：衡量业务交接的可靠性}
\subpart{The operational problem}{业务问题}
\bi{A container's physical journey and its information journey must stay aligned. Arrival notices, manifests, customs releases, yard locations, and inland schedules are produced by different actors. A correct record arriving after a truck has departed can be as costly as an incorrect record. The ICM problem therefore asks for a system that evaluates people, technologies, processes, and their connection \cite{comap}.

Our assessment covers two complete chains: import, from arrival information to inland release; and export, from inland receipt to vessel loading confirmation. A chain succeeds only when all required information is correct enough for the agreed task and available by its deadline. We do not equate analytics maturity with the number of dashboards, licenses, or sensors.}{集装箱的实物流转与信息流转必须保持一致。到港通知、舱单、海关放行、堆场位置及内陆运输计划由不同主体产生。即使记录准确，如果在卡车离港后才到达，也可能失去业务价值。ICM赛题因此要求评价人员、技术、流程及三者之间的联系\cite{comap}。

本文覆盖进口与出口两条完整业务链：前者由到港信息开始，终止于内陆交接；后者由内陆接货开始，终止于装船确认。只有全部必需信息达到约定准确性并在截止时间前可用，才视为成功。成熟度不能用仪表盘、软件许可证或传感器数量替代。}

\subpart{Methodological position}{方法选择依据}
\bi{Port digitalization connects many organizations, rather than only upgrading equipment \cite{ports}. W3C's quality vocabulary separates a quality dimension, its metric, and a measurement \cite{dqv}; provenance records explain how a result was derived \cite{prov}. These ideas motivate our indicator dictionary and audit trail, but supply no numerical port thresholds.

A static weighted score is easier to compute than a state model, but cannot separate noisy observations from delayed capability changes. Bayesian filtering provides a suitable temporal framework \cite{sarkka}. Direct chain-probability modeling avoids multiplying dependent component reliabilities. Proper scoring rules assess whether the resulting probabilities are useful \cite{scoring}. Scenario robustness then converts them into decisions with explicit uncertainty boundaries \cite{robust}.}{港口数字化需要连接多个组织，而不仅是升级设备\cite{ports}。W3C质量词汇区分质量维度、指标与测量值\cite{dqv}；溯源体系说明结果如何产生\cite{prov}。这些依据支持指标字典和审计链，但不提供港口数值阈值。

静态加权评分便于计算，却无法分离观测噪声和能力变化的延迟。贝叶斯滤波提供时序分析基础\cite{sarkka}。直接建模业务链概率，可以避免把相互依赖的组件可靠性机械相乘；适当评分规则用于检验概率质量\cite{scoring}；情景稳健优化再把这些概率转化为有明确边界的资源决策\cite{robust}。}

\pagepart{Collection protocol and confidentiality}{数据采集协议与保密设计}
\subpart{Collect the denominator as carefully as the numerator}{分母与分子同等重要}
\bi{A success rate is meaningless when failed transactions disappear from its denominator. A transaction registry is therefore created before delivery. Every expected handoff receives a business identifier, source and destination, required fields, and a deadline. Retries retain separate technical attempt identifiers but share one business identifier. Audit tests and personnel records use pseudonymous identifiers stored separately from names.

The minimum record is $(\mathrm{id},\mathrm{business\ id},b,\mathrm{event\ time},\mathrm{ingest\ time},\mathrm{source},\mathrm{value},\mathrm{quality},\mathrm{version})$. Event time assigns the reporting month; ingestion time detects lateness. A closed month has a frozen initial report and, where needed, a separately versioned restatement. A missing source record remains visible as an unknown result.}{如果失败交易从分母中消失，成功率就没有意义。应在交付之前建立预期交易清单，为每项交接记录业务编号、发送与接收方、必需字段和截止时间。技术重试保留独立尝试编号，但共享业务编号。审核与人员记录使用假名化编号，与姓名分开存储。

最小记录包含事件编号、业务编号、业务单元、事件时间、录入时间、来源、值、质量标记和规则版本。事件时间用于归属月份，录入时间用于识别迟报。月报冻结后，补录需形成独立修订版本；来源记录缺失时，仍保留为未知结果。}
\subpart{Stratified manual audits}{分层人工核验}
\bi{Logs should be collected exhaustively where practical. Manual tests are stratified by role, shift, terminal, cargo class, and workload. If $N_h$ is the stratum population and $y_h/n_h$ its audited pass rate,}{日志在可行时全量采集；人工测试按岗位、班次、港区、货种和负荷分层。设层总体为$N_h$，审核通过率为$y_h/n_h$，则}
\begin{equation}
\widehat p=\sum_h W_h\frac{y_h}{n_h},\qquad W_h=\frac{N_h}{\sum_hN_h},\qquad
\widehat{\mathrm{Var}}(\widehat p)=\sum_h W_h^2\left(1-\frac{n_h}{N_h}\right)\frac{s_h^2}{n_h}.
\end{equation}
\bi{With per-audit cost $c_h$ and estimated within-stratum standard deviation $S_h$, continuous optimal allocation satisfies $n_h\propto N_hS_h/\sqrt{c_h}$, subject to a minimum sample in every relevant stratum. Repeated employees or voyages require a cluster bootstrap instead of the independent-record variance above. \pagepart{Model I: Lag-aware Capability Filter (LCF)}{模型一：含滞后的能力滤波模型（LCF）}
\subpart{From actions to latent capability}{从行动到潜在能力}
\bi{For each dimension, let $a_{dt}=\sigma(z_{dt})$, where $\sigma$ is the logistic function. We use a persistent state with a delayed action input:}{令每维能力$a_{dt}=\sigma(z_{dt})$，$\sigma$为逻辑函数。状态同时包含持续性和延迟行动输入：}
\begin{align}
z_{dt}&=\phi_dz_{d,t-1}+(1-\phi_d)\mu_d+\beta_d\widetilde u_{dt}+\eta_{dt},&\eta_{dt}&\sim N(0,Q),\label{eq:state}\\
\widetilde u_{dt}&=\frac{\sum_{\ell=0}^{2}0.6^\ell u_{d,t-\delta_d-\ell}}{1+0.6+0.6^2},&u_{dt}&=0\text{ for }t<1.
\end{align}
\bi{Here $\delta_d$ is onset delay, distinct from the three-month decay window. Persistent action effects accumulate through the state equation. Applying another lag smoother to the same KPI would count the delay twice. The observation mechanism is}{其中$\delta_d$为起效延迟，与三个月衰减窗口不同。行动效应通过状态方程积累；如果再对同一KPI进行滞后平滑，会重复计算延迟。观测机制为}
\begin{equation}Y_{dt}\mid z_{dt}\sim\operatorname{Binomial}(N_{dt},\sigma(z_{dt}-0.25c_t)),\qquad c_t=\sin(2\pi(t-1)/12).
\end{equation}
\bi{The workload coefficient $-0.25$ and process-noise standard deviation $\sqrt Q=0.08$ are declared scenario assumptions. They are not estimates from ICM. In deployment, estimate them using a pilot and audit-based workload contrasts.}{负荷系数$-0.25$及状态扰动标准差$\sqrt Q=0.08$是公开的情景假设，不是ICM估计值。部署时需利用试点和不同负荷下的独立审核估计。}
\subpart{An explicit working approximation}{明确采用的近似方法}
\bi{For computational transparency, we fit an empirical-logit Gaussian working model, not an exact binomial filter:}{为便于复核，本文使用经验对数几率的高斯工作模型，而非精确二项滤波：}
\begin{align}
r_{dt}&=\log\frac{Y_{dt}+1/2}{N_{dt}-Y_{dt}+1/2}+0.25c_t,\\
R_{dt}&=\frac1{Y_{dt}+1/2}+\frac1{N_{dt}-Y_{dt}+1/2},\qquad r_{dt}\approx z_{dt}+\epsilon_{dt}.
\end{align}
\bi{With filtered mean $m_{t-1}$ and variance $V_{t-1}$, the scalar Kalman update is}{给定上一期滤波均值$m_{t-1}$和方差$V_{t-1}$，标量Kalman更新为}
\begin{align}
m_t^-&=\phi m_{t-1}+(1-\phi)\mu+\beta\widetilde u_t,&V_t^-&=\phi^2V_{t-1}+Q,\\
K_t&=V_t^-/(V_t^-+R_t),&m_t&=m_t^-+K_t(r_t-m_t^-),\quad V_t=(1-K_t)V_t^-.
\end{align}
\algo{1}{\bi{Estimate and forecast capabilities}{估计并预测能力}}{\bi{\textbf{Input:} monthly counts, workload, dated actions. Fit $(\phi,\mu,\beta)$ on months 1--24 by minimizing the Gaussian innovation negative log likelihood for each $\delta\in\{0,1,2,3\}$. Select delay on months 25--36. Freeze parameters, then filter and predict months 37--48 sequentially. \textbf{Output:} $\sigma(m_t)$, one-step predictions, and conditional intervals $\sigma(m_t\pm1.645\sqrt{V_t})$.}{\textbf{输入：}月度计数、负荷与行动日期。对每个$\delta\in\{0,1,2,3\}$，在1--24月最小化高斯创新负对数似然，估计$(\phi,\mu,\beta)$；用25--36月选择延迟；冻结参数后，逐期滤波并预测37--48月。\textbf{输出：}$\sigma(m_t)$、一步预测及条件区间$\sigma(m_t\pm1.645\sqrt{V_t})$。}}

\pagepart{Model II: Chain Reliability Maturity (CRM)}{模型二：业务链可靠性成熟度模型（CRM）}
\bi{Maturity should answer a concrete question: given a defined service environment, how likely is the data system to complete the customer's handoff correctly and on time? A chain-level model measures the joint event directly. It does not multiply stage success rates as if shared platforms were independent.}{成熟度应回答具体问题：在约定服务环境下，数据系统有多大概率正确、及时地完成客户交接？业务链模型直接测量联合事件，不把共享平台上的各环节视为独立后机械相乘。}
\begin{equation}
\operatorname{logit}p_k(a,c,o)=b_{0k}+b_{Pk}a_P+b_{Tk}a_T+b_{Rk}a_R+h_ka_Pa_R+\gamma_kc+\psi_ko.
\label{eq:crm}
\end{equation}
\bi{The people--process interaction represents skilled staff being able to use an assigned owner, consistent metadata, and an escalation path. $o$ indicates a shared outage, so both chains can deteriorate together. Constrain $b_{Pk},b_{Tk},b_{Rk},h_k\ge0$ and $\gamma_k,\psi_k\le0$. Then}{人员与流程交互表示：员工技能需要结合明确责任人、一致元数据及升级路径，才能转化为业务效果。$o$表示共享系统故障，使两条业务链可以同时恶化。约束$b_{Pk},b_{Tk},b_{Rk},h_k\ge0$及$\gamma_k,\psi_k\le0$，则}
\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial p_k}{\partial a_P}=p_k(1-p_k)(b_{Pk}+h_ka_R)\ge0,
\qquad \frac{\partial p_k}{\partial a_R}=p_k(1-p_k)(b_{Rk}+h_ka_P)\ge0.
\end{equation}
\bi{These inequalities prevent an estimated association from recommending deliberately worse skills or governance within the model domain. They are structural restrictions, not empirical proof that every real intervention helps.}{上述限制避免模型在适用域内建议故意降低技能或治理水平。它们属于结构约束，不能证明现实中所有干预都有效。}
\subpart{Separate comparable capability from current exposure}{区分可比能力与当前业务暴露}
\begin{equation}
M^{\rm ref}(a)=100\sum_{k=1}^{2}\omega_kp_k(a,0,0),\qquad
M^{\rm op}_t=100\sum_{k=1}^{2}\omega_kp_k(a_t,c_t,o_t),\quad\omega=(0.6,0.4).
\end{equation}
\bi{Reference maturity fixes workload and outage conditions. Operational performance retains the actual environment and must be reported alongside it. The demonstration weights follow an assumed import/export transaction mix, not loss severity; monetary loss weights appear separately in optimization. During a real outage, a stable reference score must never conceal an operational red alert.

We report a continuous score and three-dimensional capability profile. No supported level boundaries are supplied by ICM, so we do not invent five maturity grades. A future certified level would require both an empirically calibrated score threshold and mandatory security, recovery, ownership, and lifecycle checks. A high average cannot offset a failed critical gate. NIST's historical 2018 framework informs the risk-management structure, not a numeric maturity conversion \cite{nist}.}{参考成熟度固定负荷与故障状态；运营表现保留实际环境，必须同时报告。演示权重取自假设的进出口交易结构，而非损失严重度，货币损失权重在优化中另行使用。真实故障发生时，稳定的参考分数不能掩盖运营红色告警。

\pagepart{Model III: Robust Improvement Portfolio (RIP)}{模型三：稳健改进项目组合（RIP）}
\subpart{Response with delay and diminishing returns}{含延迟与边际收益递减的响应}
\bi{Let project $j$ receive level $x_j\in\{0,1,2,3\}$, with one-time start $s_j\in\{1,2\}$ when active. Its response at quarter $q$ is}{项目$j$的投入级别$x_j\in\{0,1,2,3\}$，启动季度$s_j\in\{1,2\}$。其在第$q$季度的响应为}
\begin{align}
f_{jq}^{(s)}&=\min\left\{1,\max\left(0,\frac{q-s_j-d_j-\Delta_s+1}{2}\right)\right\},\quad E_{jq}^{(s)}=x_jf_{jq}^{(s)},\\
g_j(E)&=G_j^{(s)}(1-e^{-k_jE}),\qquad g'_j>0,\quad g''_j<0,\\
a_q^{(s)}(x)&=\operatorname{clip}\left(a^{0,(s)}+\sum_j m_jg_j(E_{jq}^{(s)}),0,0.995\right).\label{eq:gain}
\end{align}
\bi{The capability increment is measured against the same baseline $a^0$ at every quarter. Adding the total cumulative gain repeatedly to the preceding quarter would create artificial growth. An equivalent recursive implementation must add only $g_j(E_{jq})-g_j(E_{j,q-1})$.}{每季度都相对于同一个基准$a^0$计算能力增量。把累计改善量反复加到上一季度能力上，会产生虚假增长。等价递推实现只能加入$g_j(E_{jq})-g_j(E_{j,q-1})$。}
\subpart{Monetary objective and feasibility}{货币目标与可行性}
\begin{equation}
Z_s(x)=\sum_{q=1}^{4}\frac{\sum_k V_{kq}L_{kq}^{(s)}[p_k(a_q^{(s)}(x),0,0)-p_k(a^{0,(s)},0,0)]}{(1+r)^{q-1}}
-\sum_{q=1}^{2}\frac{C_q^{(s)}(x)}{(1+r)^{q-1}}.
\end{equation}
\bi{Here $V$ is the quarterly count and $L$ the loss per failed handoff; their product is converted to USD 10,000 units. Safety and legal obligations are not traded against this monetary benefit. With $y_j=\mathbf1(x_j>0)$, solve}{其中$V$是季度业务量，$L$是单次交接失败损失，二者乘积换算为万美元。安全和法定义务不与货币收益交换。令$y_j=\mathbf1(x_j>0)$，求解}
\begin{align}
\max_{x,s,v}\quad &v,&v&\le Z_s(x)\quad(\forall s\in\mathcal S),\\
\sum_{j:s_j=q}\kappa_s(x_j+0.15y_j)&\le B_q,&\sum_{j:s_j=q}h_jx_j&\le H_q,\\
y_{\rm integration}&\le y_{\rm governance},&s_{\rm governance}&\le s_{\rm integration}\quad\text{if integration active},\\
x_{\rm recovery}&\ge1.&&
\end{align}
\bi{A recovery floor is an explicit scenario management decision, not a universal standard. If real acceptance must precede integration, replace this with a strict commissioning dependency. \pagepart{Evaluation, limitations, and recommendations}{模型评价、局限与建议}
\subpart{What the framework achieves}{框架能够完成什么}
\bi{The measurement dictionary connects nine business needs to inspectable fields and corrective actions. The coverage model reduces illustrative high-frequency collection effort while preserving mandatory controls. Temporal capability estimates retain a distinction between a current observation and a persistent state; chain probabilities translate that state into a customer-relevant service event. The project optimizer accounts for diminishing returns and implementation feasibility, and its finite-menu optimum can be independently verified.

The strongest feature is traceability. A reported score can be followed backward to its chain probabilities, capability estimates, audit counts, missingness decisions, and source definitions. A proposed investment can be followed forward to the conditions under which it is expected to improve the service.}{指标字典把九类业务需求连接到可检查字段与整改行动。覆盖模型在保留强制控制的同时降低示例高频采集成本。时序能力估计区分当期观测与持续状态，业务链概率再把状态转化为客户关心的服务事件。资源优化同时考虑边际收益递减和实施约束，其有限菜单最优解可独立核验。

该框架的主要价值是可追溯性：从评分可反查业务链概率、能力估计、审核计数、缺失处理及来源定义；从投资方案可追踪它在何种条件下预期改善服务。}
\subpart{Limits that materially affect use}{影响实际使用的局限}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \bi{\textbf{No enterprise validation.} All numerical parameters and results come from labeled simulations. The framework cannot assign ICM a genuine maturity score or promise a return on investment.}{\textbf{缺少企业验证。} 全部数值参数与结果来自明确标记的模拟，不能据此给ICM真实评分或承诺投资回报。}
\item \bi{\textbf{State uncertainty is undercovered.} Conditional intervals cover only 66.7\% of test states. Treating them as calibrated 90\% intervals would be misleading.}{\textbf{状态区间覆盖不足。} 条件区间仅覆盖66.7\%测试状态，不能把它们宣称为已校准的90\%区间。}
\item \bi{\textbf{Some parameters are not identified reliably.} Technology onset delay is misrecovered; coefficient shrinkage alters response curvature. More varied pilots and independent control observations are needed.}{\textbf{部分参数不能可靠识别。} 技术起效延迟恢复错误，系数收缩改变响应曲率，需要更多样化试点与独立对照观测。}
\item \bi{\textbf{Menu optimality is limited.} Only five projects, three levels, and two start quarters are searched. A zero enumeration gap is not global optimality over continuous budgets or every possible project.}{\textbf{菜单最优性有限。} 仅搜索五项目、三级投入与两个启动季度，枚举零间隙不等于对连续预算和全部可能项目全局最优。}
\item \bi{\textbf{Robustness is bounded.} Costs beyond the planning envelope cause 5\% violations in the wider test. Legal requirements, catastrophic losses, and multi-year capability decay are not monetized in this demonstration.}{\textbf{稳健性有边界。} 成本超出规划范围后，更宽测试中出现5\%违约。法律要求、灾难性损失和多年能力衰减未在演示中货币化。}
\end{enumerate}
\subpart{Recommended decision sequence}{建议的决策顺序}
\bi{ICM should first establish expected-event denominators, independent failure checks, ownership, and recovery evidence. It should then publish a provisional capability profile with missing-data bounds and current incident status. After collecting enough varied history, it can calibrate the chain probabilities and run the portfolio model with its own costs, capacity, and service requirements.

In the declared demonstration, a \PlanCost-unit portfolio raises nominal reference maturity from \BaseMaturity\ to \FinalMaturity. The actionable conclusion is to measure governance and delayed skill effects before committing to a large technology purchase. The numerical portfolio remains conditional on assumptions that have not been verified at ICM.}{ICM应先建立预期事件分母、失败独立复核、责任分工和恢复证据；随后公布包含缺失边界及当前事故状态的暂定能力画像。在积累具有足够变化的历史记录后，才校准业务链概率，并以本企业成本、容量和服务要求运行组合优化。

在明确声明的演示中，\PlanCost\ 单位组合使名义参考成熟度从\BaseMaturity\ 升至\FinalMaturity。可以落实的建议是：大规模采购技术之前，先测量治理缺口和技能改善的延迟效果。数值组合始终以尚未在ICM验证的假设为条件。}
